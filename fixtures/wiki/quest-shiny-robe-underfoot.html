Walkthrough

[edit | edit source]

Kejar the Mighty, a named imp found deep in The Hole (#9 on the map) asks you to bring him a Shining Metallic Robe. Kejar is scowls with benevolence and in elemental illusion. Blue to lvl 43 enchanter.

Indifferent to a 35 rogue with shroud of stealth AA. Which was sufficient for turn in. -

Due to Legends Pet mechanics, the "only" way to turn in is rogue level 35 AA Shroud of stealth (5 AA pts). Charming him as your OWN pet does not work (both as a bard and an ench), due to pet inventory mechanics. Having another player charm him does not work (both as a bard and an ench), due to pet inventory mechanics. Mezzing him and mind wiping him does not work, though he does appear indifferent at this time, he will not accept a turn in and return the item back.

The Shining Metallic Robes is dropped rarely off the ghoul arch magi on the dead side of Lower Guk.

In exchange for the SMR, you'll either receive either a Shiny Hunk of Metal or a Shiny Robe of the Underfoot. The former is the more common of the two.

02/10/2024: Eight turn ins yielded two Robes.
