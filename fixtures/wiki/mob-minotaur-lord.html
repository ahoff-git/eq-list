Description

Shares a spawn cycle with a minotaur slavers (with an "a", 3 spawns in the mines). He can spawn at any of their spawn points. If no Lord is up, one will spawn on the first available a minotaur slaver (with an "a") spawn inside the mines. Simply run around the mines killing the slavers, and he'll pop within 10 minutes or so.

When attacked: "Minotaur Lord says, 'I survived the gladiator pens of Qeynos!! What can you do to one such as I?!!'"

Can spawn the Minotaur Hero when killed.
