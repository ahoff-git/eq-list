Description

These giants wander the various plains of Norrath, as well as the Rathe Mountains and West Commons. Although often hunted for the plat they drop, they can be a serious problem for lower level players.

Habitats include: Western Plains of Karana, West Commonlands, Northern Plains of Karana, Rathe Mountains, Eastern Plains of Karana.

Rathe Mountain Placeholders:(may be incomplete) a cyclops, a lizard man warrior, a lizard man mystic, a basilisk, a giant skeleton, a grizzly bear, Broog Bloodbeard, Tarskuk

Western Plains of Karana: One spawn @ (-575, -13060) on a 6:40 timer. PHs are a grizzly bear, a giant spider, a black wolf, a brown bear, a mist wolf, and a fire beetle. Begins to path away immediately. Caution: Oobnopterbevny Biddilets will path near this spawn. Another giant (or PH) spawns at (298, -10126) (WHG) and immediately begins pathing away.

Eastern Karana: One spawn @ (-1157, -1030) roams from there on a 6 minute timer. Another spawn on a 6 minute timer on the eastern ridge of the same mountain as the other spawn @ (-1760, -2060), both can have five placeholders: a willowisp, a silvermist wolf, a griffawn, an evil eye, and a gorge hound. After the placeholders spawn they move randomly across the top and sides of the mountain but will return back to their spawn point. * A third giant spawns @ (-1560, -175) when both other giants have been killed, still confirming this is the case.
