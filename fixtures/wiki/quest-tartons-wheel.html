Walkthrough

[edit | edit source]

Travel to Everfrost to find Sulgar at the edge of the tundra. He's on the north wall, a bit west from the western fork of the frozen river.

You say, 'Hail, Sulgar'

Sulgar says, 'Hail, traveler! Would you happen to know the location of any of the [spokes] of Tarton's Wheel?'

You say, 'spokes'

Sulgar says, 'A pity, I shouldn't have expected you to know. Though, [Tarton's Wheel] would be a fine prize.'

You say, 'Tarton's Wheel'

Sulgar says, 'Tarton's Wheel was a device that Tarton constructed to let him travel through the planes. If you are [interested] in helping me reassemble it, I will [reward] you well.'

You say, 'reward'

Sulgar says, 'If you bring me the [Staff of the Wheel] and the [Star of Eyes], I will reward you a spell - for personal use, of course...'

You say, 'Staff of the Wheel' or You say, 'Star of Eyes'

Sulgar says, 'The Staff of the Wheel and the Star of Eyes are made by combining the ten spokes of Tarton's Wheel in a [wheel case].'

You say, 'interested'

Sulgar says, 'Tarton's Wheel was split into 10 separate pieces. These pieces were then scattered across the face of Norrath. I have [a tome] that I can give you, detailing the wheel of Tarton, as well as a [wheel case] that you can have when you are ready to assemble the pieces.'

You say, 'a tome'

Sulgar says, 'This tome details the wheel of Tarton, and the lore on where the pieces of it may be found. Use it in good faith.'

You receive Tome of the Wheel, which gives clues on where to find the pieces of the staff.

You say, 'wheel case'

Sulgar says, 'I will lend you this wheel case - put the ten spokes of the wheel in it when you have collected them. I did say lend, however. I shall need the case back. Once you have constructed the Staff of the Wheel and the Star of Eyes, I will trade you them for the knowledge of how to cast the most powerful spell I know.'

You receive Glowing Chest, a ten slot combine container that identifies as "Tartons Wheel Case."

The chest is a TEMPORARY container. Any stored items will be LOST after camping.

There are ten pieces of the staff scattered across Norrath, each called Rod. Combine them in Glowing Chest.

You have fashioned the items together to create something new: Staff of the Wheel. You have fashioned the items together to create something new: Star of Eyes.

(Despite what Sulgar said, the wheel case is destroyed when this combine is made, and he does not require it to be returned. Give Staff of the Wheel and Star of Eyes to Sulgar:

You receive Spell: Ice Comet

He will NOT return the staff or star. The trade is PERMANENT.
