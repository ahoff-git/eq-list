Walkthrough

[edit | edit source]

This is the quest to obtain the Langseax or the Langseax of the Wolves. You need at least Kindly with the Wolves of the North to start this quest.

Faction Note: This quest requires a secondary quest - Lion Meat Shipment Quest which can probably be done with much lower faction. You can also do this quest a bunch of times in one go by asking Misla for the lion meat multiple times. Take empty bags and do you can do the quest 50+ times in one trip, gaining +350 Wolves of the North faction. You can use this to boost your faction for the Langsaex quest and get plenty Lion Delight for Iceberg.

Quest Summary

[edit | edit source]
  • Complete the Lion Meat Shipment quest to get Lion Delight.
  • Run to Iceberg, give Lion Delight, receive shirt.
  • Run to Frostbite, give shirt, follow Frostbite to the criminal.
  • Kill the criminal.
  • Return criminal's head for Langseax.

Part 1: The Story

[edit | edit source]

Go through the following steps to learn the story behind this quest. However, it's unnecessary - you can skip it and go to Part 2.

  • Go to Renth McLanis in the tavern across from Mac's Kilts. Ask him "What dangerous matter?". He tells you of a crime. Agree to hunt the murderer. Renth then sends you to see Dok in Dok's Cigars. Find out all you can about the crime.
  • He tells you to go see the leader of Clan McMannus. This is the fishing village located in West Karanas. Speak to the leader. Then speak to Ulrich McMannus and he tells you Frostbite can track prey if he has a piece of sweaty clothing.
  • Return to Dok and ask him "Where is the sweaty shirt?". He then tells you he sold it to Tundra Jack. Find Tundra Jack on the tundra of Everfrost. He will tell you that Iceberg is using it for a collar and that you can distract Iceberg with his favorite meal, Lion Delight.

Short version: You need a Sweaty Shirt for Frostbite to track the murderer. The polar bear Iceberg, Tundra Jack's companion and very good boy, has the shirt. You need some Lion Delight to get the shirt.

Part 2: The Sweaty Shirt

[edit | edit source]
  • Go back to Halas and speak to Teria O`Danos. Do the Lion Meat Shipment Quest.
    • If you want to do this quest multiple times - either to merge multiple Langseax or to get Langseax of the Wolves you can do a bunch of the Lion meat quests in one go to save time. Simply ask Misla for the lion meat shipment a bunch of times.
    • WARNING: Once you have Lion Delight, be sure to bury it lower in your container than your normal food to prevent it being eaten by you.
  • Take the Lion Delight to Iceberg and receive the Sweaty Shirt and a faction adjustment.
    • Iceberg can be found roaming the tundra of Everfrost, usually west of the river. He spawns at Tundra Jack's tower (2050,-3800) and immediately runs off to find Tundra Jack. However, if you wait at the tower, the two of them return to the tower frequently.
      • You can often find him stuck in pathing in front of the tower if you hop into a difficulty instance no one else is using, like D2 or D3.
      • After giving him the meat, he immediately respawns at the tower, making it easy to hand in the meat over and over and over for faction and more shirts if you plan to complete the quest multiple times.

Part 3: The Murderers

[edit | edit source]

This step appears to be slightly broken on EQ Legends - giving Frostbite the shirt causes the murderer to spawn, but Frostbite will not track for you.

  • Return to the McMannus fishing village in West Karana and give the shirt to Frostbite.

If Frostbite responds to the shirt with "RUFF RUFF" something bugged and the murderer did not spawn. He should react to the shirt with "Frostbite takes a whiff of the sweaty shirt and barks".

The murderer will be either Paglan or Basil. Paglan is tougher to kill and probably in his low to mid 20's, Basil is around level 20. Since Frostbite won't track for you it is recommended you just a loadout that includes a class with tracking. You can try finding them on your own, they seem to spawn on the western side of the map, but then run up into the mountainous north/northeast area of the map. Typically they pass through the area north of the Ogre shaman camp (area 18 on the West Karana map). Run speed buffs are also recommended.

Give the head to Renth for faction, coin, and of course, the Langseax (if you have Basil's head) or the Langseax of the Wolves (if it is Paglan's head). I am not sure what triggers which criminal you get or even if you can just kill both and get both heads, but it seems that you wil normally get Basil.

Faction Adjustments

[edit | edit source]

When you complete the lion meat quest:

  • Shaman of Justice (+7)
  • Wolves of the North (+7)
  • Merchants of Halas (+10)
  • Rogues of the White Rose (+5)

When you trick Iceberg and get the Sweaty Shirt:

  • Shaman of Justice (+5)
  • Wolves of the North (+5)
  • Merchants of Halas (+5)
  • Steel Warriors (+5)
  • Rogues of the White Rose (+5)

When you kill Paglan or Basil:

  • Rogues of the White Rose (-5)


(Stuff after this is unconfirmed for EQL)


When you turn in Paglan's or Basil's head for your reward:

* Shaman of Justice (-1)

  • Wolves of the North (+1)
  • Merchants of Halas (-1)
  • Rogues of the White Rose (+1

Faction adjustments overall:

* Shaman of Justice (+1)

  • Wolves of the North (+3)
  • Merchants of Halas (+1)
  • Steel Warriors (+1)
  • Rogues of the White Rose (-2)
