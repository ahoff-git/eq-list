Walkthrough

[edit | edit source]

Vylleam Vyaeltor is located in the room behind King Tormax's throne room in Kael Drakkel.

You say, 'Hail, Vylleam Vyaeltor'

Vylleam Vyaeltor bows 'Greetings to you. You are far away from your homelands, stranger. I am looking for someone. Someone that the outlanders call a cleric. Are you a cleric?'

You say, 'I am a cleric'

Vylleam Vyaeltor says 'Ahhh... most pious one. I sense the aura of your devotion and conviction and it is strong about you. A shame you are so short lived, mortal. But I see potential in you and for that I shall aid you in your calling. Do you wish my aid?'

You say, 'I wish your aid'

Vylleam Vyaeltor says 'Then I have a set of goals for you. Once you have achieved them, you shall be rewarded with such as these; a helm, a breastplate, armplates, bracers, gauntlets, greaves and boots. May they protect you from your enemies.'

After handing in your items:

Vylleam Vyaeltor smiles warmly as he hands you your reward. 'You have done well, _____.'

  • Your faction standing with Kromzek got better.
  • Your faction standing with Kromrif got better.
  • Your faction standing with King Tormax got better.
  • Your faction standing with Claws of Veeshan got worse.

You gain experience!!

Helm

[edit | edit source]

You say, 'What helm?'

Vylleam Vyaeltor says 'I shall craft a helm for you but you must seek these items out for me first. I have need of an ancient tarnished plate helmet and three crushed onyx sapphires..'

Hand in an Ancient Tarnished Plate Helmet (random rare drop from Claws of Veeshan mobs in the Temple of Veeshan) and three Crushed Onyx Sapphires.

Breastplate

[edit | edit source]

You say, 'What about the breastplate?'

(Response?)

Hand in an Ancient Tarnished Breastplate (random rare drop from Claws of Veeshan mobs in the Temple of Veeshan) and three Black Marbles.

Vambraces

[edit | edit source]

You say, 'What armplates?'

Vylleam Vyaeltor says 'For the armplates, I shall require a pair of ancient tarnished plate armplates as well as three jaundice gems. Go forth and seek these out.'

Hand in an Ancient Tarnished Vambraces (random rare drop from Claws of Veeshan mobs in the Temple of Veeshan) and three Jaundice Gems.

Bracer

[edit | edit source]

You say, 'What bracers?'

Vylleam Vyaeltor says 'For the bracers, faithful one, I shall require the acquisition of an ancient tarnished plate bracer and three crushed opals.'

Hand in an Ancient Tarnished Plate Bracelet (random rare drop from Claws of Veeshan mobs in the Temple of Veeshan) and three Crushed Opals.

Gauntlets

[edit | edit source]

You say, 'What gauntlets?'

Vylleam Vyaeltor says 'I will need a pair of ancient tarnished gauntlets and three crushed lava rubies, so that I may make your reward.'

Hand in an Ancient Tarnished Plate Gauntlets (random rare drop from Claws of Veeshan mobs in the Temple of Veeshan) and three Crushed Lava Rubies.

Greaves

[edit | edit source]

You say, 'What greaves?'

Vylleam Vyaeltor says 'You shall acquire a pair of ancient tarnished plate greaves and three chipped onyx sapphires for me. I await your return.'

Hand in an Ancient Tarnished Greaves (random rare drop from Claws of Veeshan mobs in the Temple of Veeshan) and three Chipped Onyx Sapphires.

Boots

[edit | edit source]

You say, 'What boots?'

Vylleam Vyaeltor says 'With your aid I shall make a pair of fine boots for you. All that is needed is a pair of an ancient tarnished plate boots and a trilogy of crushed flame emeralds.'

Hand in an Ancient Tarnished Plate Boots (random rare drop from Claws of Veeshan mobs in the Temple of Veeshan) and three Crushed Flame Emeralds.
