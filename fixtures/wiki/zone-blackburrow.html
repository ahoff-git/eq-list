Blackburrow is the home of the Blackburrow clan of gnolls. It is something of a combination between underground dungeon and above ground hunting area, and represents the only pathway to get from Everfrost and the home of the barbarians to the Qeynos Hills and lands beyond. The overall structure of Blackburrow consists of two entrances and a ring with a deep hole in the middle. There are multiple tunnels to the lower levels where higher level gnolls can be found.

Dangers

[edit | edit source]

Blackburrow can be an extremely dangerous dungeon to adventure in for unprepared players/groups. The most obvious danger is the grouping density of gnolls which often have as many as 5 gnolls spawning within aggro range of each other. Which is made even more difficult by the wide level range within these spawn groupings as it is common to see an elite guard within a group of lower level gnolls making a clean "pull" almost impossible. Also, Blackburrow gnolls are very social. They will eagerly come to the aid of their brethren as patrolling gnolls can sneak up on you from around blind corners, and even aggro from behind doors. Be very mindful of AoE and fear-inducing spells/abilities as one errant spell or a running gnoll can trigger massive trains.

During peak times when there actually are a decent number of players in the zone (10-15 people on average) the above mentioned trains become common. Quite often parties deeper in the dungeon will miss a pull and head for the surface dragging along every gnoll in their path. The architectural challenge with Blackburrow is that there is only a single tunnel which leads to the surface, and it is along this very tunnel that most lower level players hunt. This can lead to a lot of deaths if the training party doesn't get a chance to shout out a warning.

Another possible threat most people don't consider is the zone itself. Blackburrow contains a large number of bridges, narrow pathways, ledges, and water. While the NPC pathing is decent, it can still result in accidental trains, and players falling off bridges or getting stuck in cracks. Mind your step!

Benefits

[edit | edit source]

This area, although dangerous, does provide some good benefits. First of all, the gnolls are generally pretty wealthy with the higher level ones often having several gold pieces each. Most of the gnolls you kill will either have a weapon, cloth armor, or even raw-hide armor off the higher level gnolls, all of which are worth cash. You'll find that every time you go home, you'll feel richer for having hunted there.

The other reason people hunt here is for quest items. The Blackburrow Gnoll Skin and Blackburrow Gnoll Pelt, which are the most commonly sought items, drop off of high-level gnolls here. Monks are seen here frequently hunting the skin and pelts for their Orange Headband. There are quite a few other quests that can be done here, looking for various items for use in the surrounding towns, so the place can be a hub of activity.

Probably the greatest benefit to hunting in Blackburrow for anyone are the gnoll fangs that drop commonly off of every gnoll in the zone. These can be handed in at Halas (Gnoll Bounty) and South Qeynos (Moonstones Quest) for exp at low levels, faction gains, and other rewards.

Traveling To and From

[edit | edit source]

There are two entrances to Blackburrow. The first is on the southeast segment of the "canyons" of Everfrost, which can be hard to find given that the area feels like a maze by its enormous cliff faces and snow-induced, low-visibility. The tunnel entrance is guarded by a couple gnolls, and has two more as you progress down through the tunnel. The tunnel itself is long and used to be hidden in darkness, but torches light the way to Blackburrow.

The other entrance is in the northeast corner of Qeynos Hills. There is no path to the entrance, but you'll find it through the mouth of a gnoll head-shaped cave entrance that lies east of Surefall Glade and north to the Western Karana zone lines.
