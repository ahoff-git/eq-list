Description

There are two different monsters with almost identical names: "minotaur slavers" and "a minotaur slaver". The latter are a level higher than the former.

A minotaur slaver, with an "a", has 4 spawn points, 3 of which roam past the cart at the first junction in the mines. The fourth is the Minotaur Lord's spawn point. They can spawn a Minotaur Hero when killed.
