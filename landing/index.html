EQ List

A floating loot shopping-list overlay for EverQuest Legends. Build a list, and it lights up the moment your items drop — data straight from eqlwiki.com.

Download for Windows Launch the app

Windows 10/11 · already installed? “Launch the app” opens it (or focuses it).

  • Fuzzy search for items, quests, and recipes — spelling can be rough.
  • Add a whole quest at once; items group under it on your list.
  • Always-on-top overlay flashes gold when a watched item drops.
  • See who drops an item, by zone — your current zone highlighted.
  • Screengrab lookup (Ctrl+Shift+L) and a live XP/kill session tracker.
Passive and external — reads your EQ log (turn it on in-game with /log on); no memory reading or game hooking.
